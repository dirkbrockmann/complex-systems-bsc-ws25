Lab Course - Modeling Complex Systems with Netlogo

This is the lab course section. The lab course is a two week block course that is designed to teach you how to model complex systems using computer simulations.

  • please bring a laptop
  • install the latest version of NetLogo on your laptop before the lab starts
  • bring something to drink
  • power outlets are sparse in the room, so if you want to help yourself and others bring Verteilungssteckerleisten.
