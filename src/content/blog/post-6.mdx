Imagine you have a system in which individual organisms can be sponaneously
generated out of nothing at some rate $\alpha$. When they exist,
they can die at some rate $\beta$. So, schematically two reactions
can occur

$$
\emptyset  \xrightarrow{\alpha}  X
$$

and
$$
X  \xrightarrow{\beta}  \emptyset
$$

Now try to describe this by a time continuous 1d dynamical system for the concentration
$x(t)$ of particles in your system. Be careful. Although the schematic
equations above look so symmetric, the changes in $x(t)$ that are
caused by them are different. The birth process is constant over
time whereas the death count depends on how many individuals exist.
When you have derived your dynamical system, analyse it.


## Chimera States in the Kuramoto Model


In class we discussed the **Kuramoto model** of phase coupled oscillators. Specifically,
we looked at two coupled oscillators:

$$
\dot{\theta}_1=\omega_1 + \frac{K}{2}\sin(\theta_2-\theta_1)
$$

$$
\dot{\theta}_2=\omega_2 + \frac{K}{2}\sin(\theta_1-\theta_2)
$$

Now, the generalization of this to $N$ oscillators is straightforward:

$$
\dot{\theta}_i=\omega_i + \frac{K}{N}\sum_{j=1}^{N}\sin(\theta_j-\theta_i).
$$

In this model, all oscillators interact with all other oscillators.
Below is an explorable that I also showed in class. It visualizes
the Kuramoto model for many oscillators. Your task is to find what
is known as a **chimera state**. This is a state in which some
of the oscillators are synchronized (i.e. they move around in a phase locked state)
whereas others are not synchronized (i.e. they move around incoherently).

Chimera states are difficult to find. You need to play around with two parameters:

1. The coupling strength $K$
2. The oscillators' angular speed variability.

Try to find a chimera state in which only one or two single oscillators aren't synchronized.
When you found such a state, take screenshots that provide evidence that you did. You need to figure
out how you can accommplish that. 

1. _Hint:_ You need to submit two pictures.
2. _Hint:_ It helps to use the co-moving reference frame feature.

## When the Kuramoto Model becomes Psychedelic


Another well studied version of the Kuramoto model is a system in which 
oscillators are arranged on a square lattice, and each oscillator is 
interacting only with its immediate neighbors.
