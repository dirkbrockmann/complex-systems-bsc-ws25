$\dot{n}_{2}=\beta n_{2}$ with positive growth rates $\alpha,\beta>0$. 

a) What are the solutions to these differential equations?

b) Now define the population frations $x_{1}(t)=n_{1}(t)/(n_{1}(t)+n_{2}(t))$ and $x_{2}(t)=n_{2}(t)/(n_{1}(t)+n_{2}(t))$. Clearly $x_{1}+x_{2}=1$. Derive differential equations for the fractions, so $\dot{x}_{1}=f_{1}(x_{1},x_{2})$
$\dot{x}_{2}=f_{2}(x_{1},x_{2})$

c) Because $x_{1}+x_{2}=1$ you can simplify the 2d system to a 1d system by letting $x=x_{1}$ and substituting $x_{2}=1-x$. Derive the dynamical system for $\dot{x}=f(x)$

d) What are the fixpoints of this system and their stability for $\alpha>\beta$?
