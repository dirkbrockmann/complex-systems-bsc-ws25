- create a network of $N$ disconnected nodes
- a fully connected networks would have $N(N-1)/2$ links
- for each of the possible links, add it with probability $p\in [0,1]$ a link
- this produces a totally random network with multiple components
- the mean degree is given by:
<EQ expr="\langle k \rangle = p(N-1) \approx pN" size="md"/>
- the degree distribution is binomial, a Poisson distribution for large $N$:
<EQ expr="P(k) \sim p^k (1-p)^{N-1-k} \approx \frac{e^{-\langle k \rangle} \langle k \rangle^k}{k!}" size="md"/>
