- Volunteers were given a packet addressed to a specific target person (a stranger), typically in another city.
- **Instruction:** They could only send the packet to someone they personally knew (first-name basis).
- **Rule:** They were not allowed to send it directly to the target unless they knew them personally.
- **Strategy:** Each sender chose the acquaintance they believed was most likely to be closer to the target.
- **Tracking:** Each participant added their name before forwarding, creating a chain.
- **Stopping condition:** The process ended when the packet reached the target (or was dropped).
- **Outcome:** Successful chains were surprisingly short—about five to six steps.
