**A Complex Network:** Networks consist of nodes and links. OFten some nodes have more connections that others. Many real networks,
biological, social or technological, exhibit complex connection patterns that can be analyzed using network science.
</Figure>


This lecture offers a condensed version of network science, the analysis and exploratation of complex networks as they appear in many natural,
social, and technological systems. We will cover some basic concepts of network theory and explore a few applications in biology.

The foundation of many complex systems are networks, nodes of interacting elements. The goal of network science is to understand these underlying 
structures better, to quantify their properties and with that information to understand dynamic processes that unfold on these networks or are
driven by them.
