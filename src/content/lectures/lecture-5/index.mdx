In this lecture, we will explore critical phenomena and tipping points in complex systems.
In the past lectures we saw that some systems undergo abrupt changes in their behavior when a system
parameter is slowly varied and crosses a critical value. For example in the Kuramoto model we saw that
when when the ratio of the coupling strength and the diference in natural frequencies passes a critical
value synchronization sets in abruptly. In the SIS epidemic model we saw that an endemic state is stable
only when the basic reproduction rate is larger than one.

In this lecture we will focus on what happens in such systems when they are close to their
critical point. We will also discuss systems that, for some reason, self-organize to be close to their critical point,
a phenomenon called self-organized criticality.
