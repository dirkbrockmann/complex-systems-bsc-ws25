This lecture is all about understanding self-similar structures in biology and fractals.
We will go over a number of examples that clearly show the abundance of self-similarity in natural systems and
explain how these naturally arise in many growth processes.

We will learn about a number of simple models that can generate patterns similar to those found in nature.
Finally, we will discuss how to characterize fractal patterns by their fractional dimensions, a natural extension
of the usual notion of dimensions (e.g. 1-dimensional lines, 2-dimensional surfaces, and 3-dimensional volumes).
