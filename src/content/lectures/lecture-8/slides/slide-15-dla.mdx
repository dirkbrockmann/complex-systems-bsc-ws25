# Diffusion Limited Aggregation revisited

<TwoCol 
  ratio="1:2"
  class="w-full p-4"
>
<div slot="left">
 - If a tip forms at the periphery, the tip is more likely to grow.
 - At the tip the branching probability is also higher.
 - Since the tip is more exposed to incoming particles, the tip grows faster.
 - This leads to a "rich get richer" effect, where the tips grow faster and faster.
 - a structure on the outside casts a "shadow" on the inside, preventing growth there.
 - This leads to fractal, highly branched structures.
