# Coorperative Growth in Bacterial Colonies

<TwoCol 
  ratio="1:2"
  class="w-full p-12"
  leftClass="text-xl"
>
<div slot="left">
- Microbes in this model are motile particles that move around 
in a two-dimensional petri dish that contains a substrate with nutrients. 
- Microbes consume the nutrients. The acquired energy is 
used for moving around in the substrate and for replication.
- In the occupied area, microbes can move around relatively easily 
and slowly deplete the nutrients. 
- To expand microbes need to push the boundary outwards into regions 
with more nutrients. The difficulty of this is controlled by the 
Medium Resistance slider.
- how efficiently the microbes metabolize the nutrients can also be controlled.
- When microbes deplete the nutrients and locally run out of food, 
they will eventually stop moving and enter a resting state.
</div>
