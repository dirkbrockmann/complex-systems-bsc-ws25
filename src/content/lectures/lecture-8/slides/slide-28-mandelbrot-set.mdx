# The Mandelbrot Set

<TwoColumn ratio="1:2" class="max-w-[100%] mx-auto my-12">
<div slot="left">

- The Mandelbrot set is defined by the equations:
$$
x_{n+1} = x_n^2-y_n^2 + a
$$
$$
y_{n+1} = 2x_ny_n + b
$$
for an initial point $(x_0, y_0) = (0, 0)$ and varying parameters (a, b).

- The question: for what combinations of (a, b) does the sequence (xn, yn) remain bounded (i.e., does not diverge to infinity)?
