# The Schnakenberg Model

<TwoCol class="mb-6 max-w-full w-full mx-auto" align="center">
<div slot="left">
<Eq expr="\partial_t u =  \alpha - u + u^2 w" size="lg" />
<Eq expr="\partial_t w =  \beta - u^2 w" size="lg" />

- species $U$ decays spontaneously
- and self-activtates regulated by species $V$
- Species $V$ gets removed by $U$
- both species are supplied (parameters $\alpha,\beta$) 
</div> 
<div slot="right">

One can show that this system has a stable equilibrium:

<Eq expr="u^\star =  \alpha + \beta" size="lg" />
<Eq expr="w^\star =  \frac{\beta}{(\alpha + \beta)^2}" size="lg" />

This euilibrium is usually approached by damped oscillations.

</div>
</TwoCol>
