# Reaction Diffusion Systems


<TwoCol class="mb-6 max-w-full w-full mx-auto" align="center">
<div slot="left">

the variables are fields

<Eq expr="u=u(x,t)\qquad w=w(x,t)" size="xl" />

the particles at/around location $x$ interact 
nonlinearly and change the concentrations

<Eq expr="\partial_t u = f(u,w)" size="xl" />
<Eq expr="\partial_t w = g(u,w)" size="xl" />

</div>

<div slot="right">

The concentrations of each type of species/particle change
because of diffusion:


<Eq expr="\partial_t u = D_u \partial_x^2 u" size="xl" />
<Eq expr="\partial_t w = D_w \partial_x^2 w" size="xl" />

putting things together we get:


<Eq expr="\partial_t u = D_u \partial_x^2 u + f(u,w)" size="xl" />
<Eq expr="\partial_t w = D_w \partial_x^2 w + g(u,w)" size="xl" />


</div>
</TwoCol>
