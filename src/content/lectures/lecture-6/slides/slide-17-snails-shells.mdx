Some species of snails and sea shells exhibit 
complex patterns on their surface. How these emerge 
and can be classified is discussed in a great 
book by [**Hans Meinhardt**](https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hans_Meinhardt_(Naturwissenschaftler)#cite_note-tib-15020-6): The Algorithmic Beauty of Sea Shells.
