# Orientation Maps in Visual Cortex

<TwoCol class="mb-6 max-w-full mx-auto p-20" align="center">
<div slot="left">
Some cells in the visual cortex exhibit an **orientation preference**, 
they respond primarily to visual stimuli on the retina that are 
oriented in a specific direction. When the entire visual 
cortex is mapped and the orientation preference mapped 
in color for many species we see an orientation map. 
This map exhibits characteristic pinwheels, singularities 
in the orientation preference.
