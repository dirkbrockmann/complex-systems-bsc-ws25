# Ocular Dominance Columns

<TwoCol class="mb-6 max-w-full mx-auto p-20" align="center">
<div slot="left">
Cells in the visual cortex exhibit an **ocular dominance**. 
They exhibit the tendency to respond to stimuli presented to 
one eye more than those presented to the other eye. 
As a function of location on the visual cortex the ocular 
dominance patterns consists of stripes of a specific wave-length.
