# Pattern formation in the Brain

<TwoCol class="mb-6" align="center">
<div slot="left">
**Optical pathways in the brain:** Each eye splits the visual 
field into left/right hemispheres. The axonal fibers from each 
of the two eyes and two hemispheres get distributed to the 
left/right visual cortices in the back of the brain, such that 
the left visual cortex receives information from the left visual 
field (from both eyes) and the right visual cortex from the right visual field.
