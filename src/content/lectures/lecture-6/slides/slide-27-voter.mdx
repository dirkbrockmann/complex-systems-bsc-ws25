# Postive Lateral interactions

## The Voter Model

The original voter model is a discrete time model 
on a square lattice. Each lattice point has one of $M$ states:
 

<Eq expr="u_{ij} = m\qquad\text{with}\qquad m \in \{1, \ldots, M\}" size="xl" />


## These are the rules for the voter model


1. The system (square lattice) is initialized in some random configuration.
2. Pick a random lattice site
3. Pick one of its 8 neighbors at random
4. The chosen site adopts the state of the chosen neighbor
5. Go back to step 1
