This lecture is about patterns in nature and how they come about, how they
naturally emerge by mechanisms that will be discussed in this lecture.
The lecture will provide many examples of patterns. Mostly we will be discussing
two-dimensional patterns, so two-dimensional structures that vary in some quantity,
e.g. color in animal coats that possess some sort of order, or patterns of
selectivity in the cortex of brains. We will learn about static patterns
that spontaneously emerge from more or less uniform initial conditions and
we will learn about the Turing mechanism, a counterintuitive example of how diffusion can destabilize
uniform states and lead to the emergence of stable patterns. We will see how spiral patterns,
like those we saw in a lattice of pulse coupled oscillators emerge and how generic these patterns are.
