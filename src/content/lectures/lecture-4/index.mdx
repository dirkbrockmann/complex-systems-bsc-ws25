In this lecture, we will explore the phenomenon of synchronization 
in natural systems. Synchronization is one of the most fundamental processes
in nature that drive the emergence of order from a disordered state. 
So in some sense it's the opposite of chaos.

Synchronization occurs everywhere, in biological systems, physical systems, 
social systems, and technological systems, and on all scales, ranging
from intracellular processes in living cells, to the scale of galaxies.

The first descriptions goes back to the Dutch scientist Christiaan Huygens
in the 17th century, who observed that two pendulum clocks mounted on the same wall
would eventually synchronize their swings.
