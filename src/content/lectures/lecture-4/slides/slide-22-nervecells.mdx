# Neuronal Synchronization

<TwoCol class="mb-6 pt-6" align="center">
  <div slot="left">
Nerve cells are an example of pulse-coupled oscillators. 
When the emit action potentials to other neurons, 
the receiving neuros change their membrane potential and their 
internal phase that determines when they emit a spike themselves. 
By this process neuros can attain a synchronous state.
