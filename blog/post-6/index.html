Making a Model for Spontaneous Life and Death

Imagine you have a system in which individual organisms can be sponaneously generated out of nothing at some rate α\alphaα. When they exist, they can die at some rate β\betaβ. So, schematically two reactions can occur

∅→αX\emptyset \xrightarrow{\alpha} X∅α​X

and

X→β∅X \xrightarrow{\beta} \emptysetXβ​∅

Now try to describe this by a time continuous 1d dynamical system for the concentration x(t)x(t)x(t) of particles in your system. Be careful. Although the schematic equations above look so symmetric, the changes in x(t)x(t)x(t) that are caused by them are different. The birth process is constant over time whereas the death count depends on how many individuals exist. When you have derived your dynamical system, analyse it.

Chimera States in the Kuramoto Model

In class we discussed the Kuramoto model of phase coupled oscillators. Specifically, we looked at two coupled oscillators:

θ˙1=ω1+K2sin⁡(θ2−θ1)\dot{\theta}_1=\omega_1 + \frac{K}{2}\sin(\theta_2-\theta_1)θ˙1​=ω1​+2K​sin(θ2​−θ1​) θ˙2=ω2+K2sin⁡(θ1−θ2)\dot{\theta}_2=\omega_2 + \frac{K}{2}\sin(\theta_1-\theta_2)θ˙2​=ω2​+2K​sin(θ1​−θ2​)

Now, the generalization of this to NNN oscillators is straightforward:

θ˙i=ωi+KN∑j=1Nsin⁡(θj−θi).\dot{\theta}_i=\omega_i + \frac{K}{N}\sum_{j=1}^{N}\sin(\theta_j-\theta_i).θ˙i​=ωi​+NK​j=1∑N​sin(θj​−θi​).

In this model, all oscillators interact with all other oscillators. Below is an explorable that I also showed in class. It visualizes the Kuramoto model for many oscillators. Your task is to find what is known as a chimera state. This is a state in which some of the oscillators are synchronized (i.e. they move around in a phase locked state) whereas others are not synchronized (i.e. they move around incoherently).

Chimera states are difficult to find. You need to play around with two parameters:

  1. The coupling strength KKK
  2. The oscillators’ angular speed variability.

Try to find a chimera state in which only one or two single oscillators aren’t synchronized. When you found such a state, take screenshots that provide evidence that you did. You need to figure out how you can accommplish that.

  1. Hint: You need to submit two pictures.
  2. Hint: It helps to use the co-moving reference frame feature.

When the Kuramoto Model becomes Psychedelic

Another well studied version of the Kuramoto model is a system in which oscillators are arranged on a square lattice, and each oscillator is interacting only with its immediate neighbors.

Below is another explorable for that. In it each lattice site is an oscillator and the color in the spectrum indicates the phase of each oscillator. When you start the simulation the coupling parameter KKK is zero. Increase it slowly and observe what happens.

Describe what you observe. What generic features do you observe? What features are stable with respect to changing parameters? What do you observe when you wait a long time?
