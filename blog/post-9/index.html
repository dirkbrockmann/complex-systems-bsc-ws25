Homework Assignment 4 (due Dec 16, 2025)

Power Laws

In class we discussed some properties of systems that are critical. One property was the natural occurance of power laws in elements of a dynamical system, we discussed the distribution of

  • avalanche sizes
  • earthquake sizes
  • severity of terrorist attacks.

Power laws have the shape:

p(x)∼1xαp(x)\sim \frac{1}{x^\alpha}p(x)∼xα1​

where the exponent α>0\alpha>0α>0 defines the law.

In this assignment you are supposed to discuss the topic with an AI, e.g. chatGPT. Ask the AI for other examples of natural, social and technological systems that exhibit power laws. Ask the AI which ones might be the result of self-organized criticality.

Once you are convinced the AI pointed you in the right direction find two examples of empirical data, e.g. plots, on the internet that illustrate the power law in the given systems.

Explain why you think the phenomenon you found could be the result of self-organized criticality.
