Assignment 2: The logistic map, period doubling and chaos

In the second lecture we introduced the logistic map:

xn+1=λ(1−xn)xnxn∈[0,1],λ∈[0,4]x_{n+1}=\lambda(1-x_{n})x_{n}\qquad x_n\in [0,1], \lambda \in [0,4]xn+1​=λ(1−xn​)xn​xn​∈[0,1],λ∈[0,4]

based on a simple, discrete time model for reproduction, growth, and dynamics of a population of a species in an ecological context. We discussed how the behavior of the model depends on the key growth parameter λ\lambdaλ, how fixpoints can be computed, how they loose stability, that period doubling occurs and, finally, when a critical λ≈3.569946...\lambda\approx 3.569946...λ≈3.569946... is reached the dynamics becomes aperiodic and chaotic and therefore appear to be unpredicatable. We also discussed that this implies sensitivity to initial conditions.

A: The Bifurcation diagram

Revisit the bifurcation diagram we discussed in class. In the bifurcation diagram, you can see the period doubling events at the sequence of critical values of the growth parameter λ\lambdaλ. We discussed in class that the distance (λn−λn−1\lambda_n-\lambda_{n-1}λn​−λn−1​) between successive period doubling events becomes smaller and smaller so that eventually, at some point the period is infinite, after an infinite amount of period doubling events. Beyond that point (λ≈3.569946...\lambda\approx 3.569946...λ≈3.569946...) all sequences of the map are aperiodic.

  1. Using the interactive bifurcation diagram, identify period doubling bifurcation points λ1\lambda_1λ1​, λ2\lambda_2λ2​, λ3\lambda_3λ3​ and λ4,...\lambda_4, ...λ4​,... and make a table of them.

  2. Compute the ratios:

δn=λn−λn−1λn+1−λn\delta_n = \frac{\lambda_{n} - \lambda_{n-1}}{\lambda_{n+1} - \lambda_{n}}δn​=λn+1​−λn​λn​−λn−1​​

This ratios is named the Feigenbaum constant (see Lecture Notes on the Logistic Map).

  1. Inspect the bifurcation diagram closely. Way beyond the critical λ≈3.569946...\lambda\approx 3.569946...λ≈3.569946..., in the regime of aperiodic dynamics, you can see bands and gaps of less complexity. What is going on there? Explain what the logistic map behaves like here, and why it is puzzling.
